# Workstreams

> Human-facing index for active work. Keep titles plain English, 9 words or fewer, and free of Task Master IDs, Trello IDs, Jira IDs, or source IDs.

| Workstream | Latest Outcome | Completed Outcome | Open Items | Recommended Next 3 | Status |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Workstream title](workstreams/workstream-slug.md) | [Result, decision, or current state] | [Done item + date/source] | [Count or top open ask] | [3 short actions] | [Today/Next/Later/Follow Up/Triage] |

## Operating Rules

- Map Outlook, Teams, Slack, Calendar, manual transcript, Quill, Granola, and local packet evidence to this list before creating a new visible workstream.
- Keep internal IDs in workstream detail files under Agent Refs, not in visible titles.
- Preserve completed outcomes and checked checklist items with completion date/source.
- Ask for confirmation before checking off an item when evidence only implies completion.
- Keep the recommended next 3 actions short, concrete, and current.
